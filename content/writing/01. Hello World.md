Hello there. Please pardon this inbox interruption. You signed up to receive emails from me at some point, but I never sent you anything. I apologize.

I'm starting a subscription-based email newsletter and wanted to give you a heads-up before emails arrive in your inbox.

The best way to consider a subscription-based email newsletter is to compare it to blog posts or podcasts. But instead of going to my website each time I write a new post, you'll find it in your email inbox.

The newsletter will feature art, photography, and my thoughts on various topics. For now, it will function as a personal blog, but it may expand to cover local issues and editorials.

If you wish to unsubscribe, I understand, and my feelings won't be hurt if you decide to go. You should be able to find the unsubscribe link below.

Please email me with recommendations and comments, or to say hello. You can reach me at [me@chrispugh.com](mailto:me@chrispugh.com).
